1. Parties and purpose

This non-disclosure agreement (NDA) is between Daniel Singh, trading as or acting on behalf of Orbital Trance Records (the "Owner"), and the individual signing below (the "Tester"). The purpose is to permit confidential evaluation of the Flowbook Lyrics / MC Flowbook application ("App").

2. Confidential information

Confidential information includes non-public builds, functionality, designs, roadmaps, source code, test credentials, unreleased images, recordings, documentation, tester communications and information clearly identified as confidential, whether communicated in writing, electronically or verbally.

3. Tester's obligations

The Tester will use confidential information only to evaluate the App; not disclose it to other people without the Owner's written permission; not post or share screenshots, videos or builds publicly; use reasonable care to protect it; and promptly inform the Owner if there is unauthorised access or disclosure. The Tester will not redistribute test copies or reverse-engineer the App, except to the extent permitted by applicable law.

4. Exclusions

The obligations do not apply to information that was already lawfully known without confidentiality restrictions, becomes public without breach, is independently developed without using confidential information, or is lawfully received from an unrestricted third party. Legally required disclosures are permitted. Nothing in this agreement restricts protected disclosures to authorities, the reporting of suspected unlawful conduct or any other rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

5. Ownership and feedback

All rights to the App and provided materials remain with their respective owners. No licence is granted except the limited right to test the App. Tester may provide optional feedback; the Owner may use that feedback to improve the App without payment, but does not acquire ownership of the Tester's separately created lyrics, recordings or other original content.

6. Duration and deletion

The Tester will keep the information confidential during testing and for two years after the last disclosure. Any information legally qualifying as a trade secret remains protected for as long as it retains that status under applicable law. Upon request, the Tester will stop testing and delete or return confidential test materials, except where retention is required by law.

7. No guarantee and governing law

Pre-release software is provided for evaluation and may contain errors. The parties intend this agreement to be legally binding. It is governed by the laws of England and Wales, subject to any mandatory protections that apply.

8. Electronic signatures

The Tester agrees that entering their name, ticking the consent box and drawing a signature is intended as electronic acceptance of this agreement. Each party should retain a complete dated copy. Electronic signatures are generally capable of legal effect, but identity, consent and the evidence retained may affect enforceability.
